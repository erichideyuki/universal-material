A FAB that opens a menu of related actions. Name the toggle with `aria-label` on the `u-fab-menu` (it has no visible text); the toggle exposes `aria-expanded`. The menu closes when an item is clicked, on Escape (focus returns to the toggle) and on a click outside it, and its items are inert while closed. Control it with the `open` attribute or `show()`, `close()` and `toggle()`, and listen to `open`/`close` (cancelable) and `opened`/`closed`, like `u-menu`.
